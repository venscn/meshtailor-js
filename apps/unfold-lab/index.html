Cutting and unfolding

Generation uses geometry only, without original UVs, seams or island hints. Default loading and Generate baseline use the same generator.
General unfolding · spatial groups and internal seams Geometry groups and UV islands are distinct. General surfaces unfold without band templates; failed candidates receive necessary cuts. Structure templates · deliberate seams 1 keeps the complete strip; 5 divides it into five pieces for square-page packing. Length and width come from geometric tube length and circumference. Only complete periodic sections qualify; other models are not forced rectangular. Regenerate to apply cuts; fill adds no seams.

Flatten verified profile connections without forcing cones, teeth or arbitrary surfaces into rectangles.

Preprocessing / postprocessing
Stitching removes seams and resolves charts; repacking does not reduce island count. Only current geometry-generated results are edited. New UVs need painting or baking.
Fill vacancies · largest islands first
The default density-spread limit is 3×. Try larger islands first, then smaller ones if they cannot fit. Compare with 1.6×. Presets only edit settings; they do not calculate or shrink existing islands. Common scaling and density spread have separate limits. Try extra angles when right angles fail. Failed large islands do not block smaller ones; budget exhaustion does not prove no space remains. Only translate, rotate and uniformly enlarge within limits. Preserve seams and do not shrink other islands. A 1.6× area gain means √1.6× edge length. Single pages only; repack multiple pages first. Invalid generated results are rejected.

Area and spatial neighbors

Strict equal density by default. A 2× area gain means √2× edge length. All islands share a common scale.
Preserve planar teeth and holes. Curved surfaces use a free boundary, not a final circular constraint. Stitching prefers the existing shape and keeps separate islands beyond the limit. This is not semantic recognition.

UV islands

Select a UV island, then a triangle inside it. Click that triangle again to clear it. Shift adds or removes islands.

Playback and display

Waiting islands stay assembled; completed islands stay at their UV target. Islands do not all start together. The queue follows descending original 3D area; click order does not affect playback.
Motion toleranceMeasured from vertices and hinge transforms, independently of camera and occlusion. Absolute tolerance: 1e-8 normalized units.

Active island · visibility
Driven by animation progress and speed; scrubbing/reversing reproduces the fade. Stationary progress stops fading. Whole-island transparency only. Hold the arrival color before fading, without blocking the next island. Pausing, finishing or releasing the scrubber restores normal display.
Legacy sphere parameters (legacy mode only)
Legacy sphere mode applies only during playback or scrubbing. Pausing restores display; model and camera remain unchanged.
